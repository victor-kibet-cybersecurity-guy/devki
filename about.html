About DEVKI GROUP OF COMPANIES

Discover the companies, industries, manufacturing network and regional presence behind one of East Africa's diversified building-materials portfolios.

Our story

A diversified manufacturing portfolio

Devki Group's published portfolio includes steel manufacturing, cement, roofing sheets, agricultural inputs, industrial packaging, aviation and regional manufacturing interests. The group operates through specialized companies serving construction, industrial and agricultural markets.

Our Chairman

Dr. Narendra Raval is identified by current Devki Group material as Chairman. This distributor site does not publish an extended executive biography.

Our companies

Key names in the published portfolio include Devki Steel Mills Limited, National Cement Company Limited, Maisha Mabati Mills Limited, Maisha Minerals & Fertilizers, Maisha Packaging Company Limited, Northwood Aviation Agencies Limited, CIMERWA PLC and CEMTECH.
